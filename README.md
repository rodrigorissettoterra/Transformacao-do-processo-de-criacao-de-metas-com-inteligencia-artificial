# AI-Assisted Goal Setting Process Transformation

> **A solution-design case exploring how Artificial Intelligence can support the creation, validation, and management of organizational goals while preserving human oversight.**

This repository documents an **AI transformation proposal** developed from a business case. The work focuses on understanding the original process, identifying opportunities for AI assistance, and proposing a more structured workflow for creating and improving organizational goals.

## Business challenge

Goal-setting processes often depend on multiple rounds of interpretation, revision, alignment, and validation.

When goals are poorly structured, organizations may face problems such as:

- ambiguous objectives;
- inconsistent wording;
- difficulty defining measurable outcomes;
- excessive manual review;
- lack of standardization between teams;
- weak traceability between business needs and final goals.

The central question of this case is:

> **How can AI improve the goal-creation process without replacing the people responsible for the final business decision?**

---

## Proposed approach

The proposal treats AI as an **assistance and quality-support layer**, not as an autonomous decision maker.

```text
Business Need
     ↓
Initial Goal Definition
     ↓
AI-Assisted Analysis
clarity · consistency · structure
     ↓
Suggested Improvements
     ↓
Human Review
     ↓
Validated Goal
     ↓
Monitoring and Revision
```

This design preserves human accountability while using AI to reduce repetitive work and improve consistency.

## Design principles

### AI as decision support

The model should assist users with analysis and suggestions, while final decisions remain under human responsibility.

### Structured input and output

A useful AI workflow should reduce ambiguity by defining what information enters the system and what type of output is expected.

### Human validation

Generated suggestions should be reviewed before becoming official organizational goals.

### Traceability

The process should make it possible to understand the original request, the AI-assisted suggestions, and the final approved version.

### Governance

An organizational AI solution should consider access, data handling, accountability, and appropriate limits on automation.

## Project scope

This is a **solution-design and transformation case**.

It documents how an AI-assisted process could be structured, but the repository does not contain a production implementation of the proposed platform.

That distinction is intentional: the project demonstrates the stage that comes before implementation — understanding the problem, defining the operating model, identifying risks, and designing an appropriate use of AI.

---

## From concept to implementation

A production evolution of the proposal could include layers such as:

```text
User Interface
      ↓
Workflow / Orchestration
      ↓
AI Assistance Layer
      ↓
Validation Rules
      ↓
Human Approval
      ↓
Persistence / Audit Trail
      ↓
Monitoring
```

Additional implementation concerns would include authentication, authorization, prompt and model versioning, evaluation, observability, privacy, and cost control.

---

## Limitations

- the repository contains a proposal rather than a deployed system;
- no production model or API is included;
- expected benefits would need to be validated through implementation and measurement;
- AI quality would require formal evaluation before operational use;
- organizational adoption and governance are as important as model capability.
